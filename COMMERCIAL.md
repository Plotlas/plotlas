research, teaching, and use by universities, non-profits, and government and public
institutions — no permission or payment needed.

**Commercial use requires a separate commercial license.** If your use of Plotlas is
directed toward commercial advantage — for example, using it inside a for-profit product
or service, in paid client work, or in a company's revenue-generating operations — reach
out and we'll set you up.

Not sure which side of the line you're on? Some uses sit in a gray area (a startup, a
corporate research lab, a consultancy). Just ask — we're friendly, and we'd rather have
the conversation than leave you guessing.
